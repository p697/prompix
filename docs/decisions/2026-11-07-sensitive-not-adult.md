# Suggestive means a sexualized image, not a woman in normal clothes

2026-11-07. Reviewing the 618 live items labelled suggestive, the owner found many plainly ordinary: women in everyday or fashion clothing, a bare midriff, shorts, normal framing. She said treating that as suggestive is too strict and reads as discriminating against women. Suggestive now needs a sexualized image: underwear or swimwear as the focus, a low or close framing that singles out the chest, buttocks or crotch, or an erotic pose or expression. Everyday, sports and fashion clothing that shows skin is general when framing and pose are ordinary; when unsure for a normally dressed person, general.

We have no adult content: explicit material is never published. The app's toggle is renamed from 显示成人内容 to 显示敏感内容 (the product session owns that string).

`npm run pipeline -- relabel` judges every live suggestive item again under this standard. The new label may loosen a live one, which the daily run never does on its own; it holds while the item's prompt and media are unchanged. A possible minor next to suggestive styling becomes explicit and leaves the library. On a sample, the owner's twelve examples came back eleven general; twelve random items came back three general, eight suggestive and one removed.
