# Detail hero shows more of tall work; the top veil is frosted, not painted

The owner found that the second image of a tall gallery opened with its head cut off. The first image was top-aligned, but later pages sat in a flexible frame that grew to the filled image and was centred by the page view. Every page now uses a fixed frame aligned to the top, as the first one does.

Tall work now shows up to 2:3 instead of 3:4. The bottom fade into the page is 40 pt with an eased curve instead of a flat 72 pt, so more of the picture shows on the first screen.

The owner also wanted the top veil lighter and finer, like frosted glass rather than something laid over the work. `EdgeVeil` used one material under an 80% paper gradient. It now stacks three ultra-thin material layers that fade out at different depths, so blur is strongest at the edge and gone at the bottom. Over work it adds only 20% paper at the very top. The held band behind a collapsed title stays opaque enough to read.
